# CDC & RDC Structural Verification

Multi-clock designs fail silently in simulation when signals or asynchronous resets cross clock boundaries without proper synchronization. SiliFlow embeds a structural **Clock Domain Crossing (CDC)** and **Reset Domain Crossing (RDC)** explorer powered by Yosys RTLIL netlist extraction.

---

## 1. Opening the CDC & RDC Explorer

- Click the **CDC / RDC** tab in the bottom panel.
- Or select **Run → Quick Open Panels → Structural CDC & RDC Explorer** / **View → Bottom Panels → CDC / RDC**.
- Try it immediately on the built-in **Dual-Clock CDC & RDC Lab** example (`cdc_demo.v`).

---

## 2. Automated Clock Domain Graph

SiliFlow parses elaborated Yosys netlists to detect every clock network (`ClockDomainInfo` with flip-flop counts and derived/parent clock relationships) and builds an interactive **Source Clock × Destination Clock Matrix Heatmap**. Clicking any matrix cell filters crossings strictly to that clock pair.

---

## 3. Five Built-In Structural Rule Checks

| Rule ID | Condition Checked | Severity |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`MISSING_SYNCHRONIZER`** | Signal crossing between unrelated clock domains without synchronization | Critical |
| **`MULTI_BIT_BUS_CDC`** | Multi-bit bus crossing domains without Gray coding, FIFO, or handshake | Critical / Warning |
| **`COMBO_BEFORE_SYNC`** | Combinational logic placed between launch flop and synchronizer chain | Critical |
| **`ASYNC_RESET_CROSSING`** | **Reset Domain Crossing (RDC)** asynchronous reset hazard across domains | Critical / Warning |
| **`SAFE_2FF_SYNC`** | Verified multi-stage synchronizer chain detected | Safe |

---

## 4. Root-Cause Guidance, Cross-Probing & Persistent Waivers

- **Code Cross-Probing**: Click any crossing row to view the full launch-to-capture path, structural explanation, RTL fix guidance, and jump straight to the **source** or **destination** flop line in Monaco.
- **Persistent Project Waivers**: Waive known static configuration signals or approved clock pairs cleanly (`siliflow:cdcWaivers:<scope>`), persisted per project across restarts.
