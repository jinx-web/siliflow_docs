# RTL Schematic Visualizer

Inspect elaborated hardware structure without leaving SiliFlow. The built-in **RTL Schematic Visualizer** pairs **Yosys** logic synthesis (with automatic `sv2v` preprocessing for SystemVerilog) and **`netlistsvg`** directly inside an editor tab.

---

## 1. Opening the Schematic

- Click **Schematic** in the top-right Action Island (or `Alt+Click` to dock it in the side split pane beside RTL code).
- Press <kbd>Ctrl+Shift+S</kbd>.
- Or select **Run → Open RTL Schematic**.

---

## 2. Canvas Controls & Multi-Module Switching

- **Zoom & Pan**: Drag anywhere to pan across the netlist, scroll to zoom smoothly, or click **Zoom In**, **Zoom Out**, and **Fit to Screen**.
- **Fullscreen View**: Toggle Fullscreen mode to inspect large multi-stage datapaths edge-to-edge (<kbd>Esc</kbd> exits).
- **Module Selector**: Switch cleanly between top-level and instantiated sub-modules in multi-module designs.

---

## 3. Cell Inspector, Resource Telemetry & Cross-Probing

- **Cell & Port Inspector**: Click any logic gate, DFF, MUX, or I/O port on the schematic to inspect cell type, parameters, connections, and attributes.
- **Yosys Resource Statistics (`parseYosysStats`)**: View wire count, wire bits, port count, port bits, total cells, and exact primitive breakdown synthesized by Yosys.
- **Source Cross-Probing (`\src`)**: SiliFlow parses Yosys `\src` location attributes (`file:line`) so clicking synthesized cells jumps straight to the originating line in your Verilog/SystemVerilog code pane.
- **Export `.svg`**: Download crisp standalone vector **SVG** netlist diagrams for lab reports, papers, and architecture docs.
