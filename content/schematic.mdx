# RTL Schematic Visualizer

Inspect elaborated and PDK-mapped hardware structure without leaving SiliFlow. The built-in **RTL Schematic Visualizer** pairs **Yosys** synthesis (`prep`, `abc`, `dfflibmap`, with automatic `sv2v` preprocessing) and **`netlistsvg`** directly inside an editor tab or a dedicated **Pop-Out OS Window**.

## 1. Opening the Schematic & Pop-Out Mode

- Click **Schematic** in the top-right Action Island (or `Alt+Click` to dock it in the side split pane beside RTL code).
- Press <kbd>Ctrl+Shift+S</kbd> or select **Run → Open RTL Schematic**.
- Click **Pop Out** (`open_schematic_window`) to detach the schematic into its own standalone OS window for multi-monitor debugging.

---

## 2. PDK & Liberty Technology Mapping

Synthesize schematics against embedded open-source standard-cell libraries (`pdk_liberty.rs`) or your own Liberty file:

- **SkyWater 130nm** (`sky130_fd_sc_hd`)
- **GlobalFoundries 180nm** (`gf180mcu_fd_sc_mcu7t5v0`)
- **IHP SG13G2 130nm** (`sg13g2_stdcell`)
- **NanGate 45nm**
- **Custom `.lib`**

---

## 3. Navigation, Search & Hierarchical Drill-Down

- **Cell & Net Search (<kbd>Ctrl+F</kbd> / <kbd>Cmd+F</kbd>)**: Search any cell instance, gate type, or net name and auto-focus/zoom straight to it on the canvas.
- **Click-to-Lock Net Tracing**: Click any wire or pin to lock the net highlight and inspect its driver and all fanout loads in the **Cell Inspector**.
- **Hierarchical Submodule Drill-Down**: Double-click any submodule block to descend into its internal gate-level schematic, with an interactive **breadcrumb trail** to step back up.
- **`Project RTL` Multi-File Elaboration Toggle**: Toggle between single-file elaboration and full multi-file project RTL elaboration.
- **Keyboard Shortcuts**: <kbd>+</kbd> / <kbd>-</kbd> (zoom), <kbd>0</kbd> (reset zoom), <kbd>F</kbd> (fit to screen), <kbd>Esc</kbd> (exit fullscreen).

---

## 4. Source Cross-Probing & Exports

- **Source Cross-Probing (`\src`)**: Clicking any synthesized cell jumps straight to the originating line in your Verilog/SystemVerilog editor.
- **Yosys Resource Statistics**: View wire count, wire bits, port count, total cells, and primitive breakdown.
- **SVG, 2x PNG & Netlist JSON Export**: Export crisp vector **SVG**, high-resolution **PNG**, or structured **Netlist JSON** diagrams for papers, reviews, and downstream tools.
