# RTL Schematic Visualizer

Visualizing hardware connections as gate-level schematics is essential for verifying synthesizer elaboration, debugging logic loops, and inspecting interconnects.

Siliflow includes a built-in **RTL Schematic Visualizer** powered by **Yosys** logic synthesis and **netlistsvg**.

---

## 1. Opening the Schematic

You can open the schematic view anytime:
- Click the **Schematic** button in the top right of the editor tab bar.
- Or click the **Schematic** pill in the top header.
- Or right-click any Verilog file in the Explorer and choose **View RTL Schematic**.

---

## 2. Interactive Schematic Canvas

The schematic canvas gives you full interactive control:

- **Pan & Zoom**:
  - Click and drag anywhere to pan the netlist canvas.
  - Scroll mouse wheel to smoothly zoom in and out.
  - Quick zoom buttons: Zoom In, Zoom Out, and Reset Zoom (100%).
- **Fit Diagram to View**:
  - Click the **Fit Diagram** button (`Crosshair` icon) to automatically scale and center the entire netlist within your screen bounds without clipping.
- **True Fullscreen Mode**:
  - Click the **Fullscreen** button (`Maximize2` icon) to expand the schematic viewer over the entire application window.
  - Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> anytime to exit fullscreen.

---

## 3. Cell & Signal Inspector

- **Click any Gate or Port**:
  Clicking on any logic gate, multiplexer, D flip-flop, or port highlights the cell in yellow and opens the **Cell Inspector** sidebar.
- **Port Connections**:
  Shows pin connections and bit vector mappings (e.g. `clk [2]`, `D [7]`, `Q [6]`).
- **Jump to RTL Source**:
  Click the **Jump to Source Code** button in the inspector to instantly open the Verilog file and highlight the exact line that generated that logic gate!
- **Interactive Wire Highlighting**:
  Hovering your cursor over any interconnect line highlights the entire net in glowing blue across all connected pins.

---

## 4. Multi-Module Selector

If your Verilog file contains multiple modules (e.g. top module, ALU, sub-counter), the clean module dropdown on the left of the toolbar lets you switch between schematics instantly without re-synthesizing.

---

## 5. Exporting High-Resolution Diagrams

Need to include your RTL schematic in a lab report, research paper, or presentation?

Click the **Download** dropdown button on the toolbar:
1. **Download SVG**: Exports a vector graphic `.svg` file that scales infinitely without pixelation.
2. **Download PNG**: Renders the schematic on a 2x high-resolution canvas with a dark background and saves a crisp `.png` image.
